Events anchored in tilset ride the sunset: flip dates and watch “Dinner” track the light while clock-anchored events stay put.
Sunrise/sunset via the NOAA solar position algorithm (USNO −0.8333° convention), computed live in your browser. Weeks, months and years keep their civil names — only their portrait changes: the week shows the light shifting, the month shows the drift, the year is one macro sun-cycle from nadir to zenith.
